Trusted-parent transcript limitation
------------------------------------

PASS for the stated threat model. The V4 protocol remains audit-provenance-based rather than tamper-proof. README.md and preregistration.json accurately bound this:

- The parent Harness is trusted to record the actual native-agent identity and transcript.
- The protocol is explicitly shared-filesystem protocol isolation.
- It is explicitly not container isolation or cryptographic agent attestation.
- Results are scoped to the fixed model, Harness, Skill snapshots, corpus, and scorer, not model-wide causal capability or production reliability.

Residual risk:
- A party able to rewrite local raw artifacts and restamp the local hash chain can rewrite transcript provenance without changing replayed pass/fail results. This is not a blocking issue under the declared trusted-parent Harness model, but it would be insufficient for an adversarial or cryptographic provenance threat model.
